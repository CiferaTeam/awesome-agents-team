# Local-first vs Cloud Tradeoffs

> What do you gain and give up by running agent tooling locally vs in the cloud?

**Status**: Stub — to be populated in Phase 1.

| Dimension | Local-first | Cloud-hosted |
|-----------|-------------|--------------|
| Data control | Full — stays on your machine | Varies by vendor |
| Collaboration | Harder — requires sync mechanism | Usually built-in |
| Setup friction | Higher | Lower |
| Cost at scale | Hardware cost | Per-seat or usage pricing |
| Offline capability | Yes | No |
| Auditability | Full local logs | Depends on vendor |
